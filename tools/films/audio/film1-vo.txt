Film 1, "Export before 16 October": voiceover
==============================================

Paste the script below into ElevenLabs as one unhurried read, and save the result as
audio/film1-vo.mp3. The film is 30 seconds long and the read must fit inside it.

Script
------

If you use Microsoft Whiteboard with a personal account, the app stops opening on the sixteenth of October. Before then, export each board: Settings, Export, Zip. SQLBI Whiteboard opens that ZIP as a board on your PC — ink, shapes, sticky notes, pictures, and comments. It is free and open source, and it keeps your boards after the deadline. The guide is at whiteboard dot sqlbi dot com.

where each phrase starts in it and move the beat starts there.
