# Microsoft Whiteboard retirement evidence

The site and the launch campaign state that Microsoft retires its standalone Whiteboard
app on **October 16, 2026**, citing
[Retirement of the standalone Microsoft Whiteboard apps](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/retirement-standalone-microsoft-whiteboard-apps).
Microsoft has repeatedly edited its published schedule, so this folder preserves the
evidence behind the claim — each revision as it stood — so it stays citable regardless
of future edits.

## Timeline of Microsoft's published schedule

**As originally published** (see
[Microsoft Whiteboard shutdown (original date).png](<Microsoft Whiteboard shutdown (original date).png>)
and the [Wayback snapshot of August 18, 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260818052606/https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/migration-of-whiteboards-from-azure-to-onedrive)):
migration ended August 22, remaining Azure boards were deleted September 5, and the
standalone apps retired **September 14, 2026** — the closing row of the schedule table.

**Late August 2026:** the migration page slipped the migration deadlines by roughly a
month (migration end August 22 → September 25; deletion September 5 → October 16;
service shutdown November 30 added) while September 14 held, demoted to a passing
sentence under "Migration trigger and eligible tenants".

**August 28, 2026:** a dedicated retirement page appeared at the URL above, postponing
the app retirement itself: "The standalone Microsoft Whiteboard apps for Windows, iOS,
and Android retire on **October 16, 2026**." It also states that after that date,
"already-installed apps display a retirement experience instead of opening the
Whiteboard gallery". October 16 is also the date remaining Azure-based boards are
permanently deleted.

## Citing the retirement publicly

- Always scope the claim to the standalone **app**: Whiteboard itself remains available
  in Teams and on the web.
- Say the app **"retires"** on October 16 — and, quoting Microsoft, that installed apps
  then display "a retirement experience instead of opening". Both are verbatim claims;
  do not paraphrase beyond them.
- Quote with a screenshot, and prefer an archived copy or the bitmap over the live
  pages: the schedule changed three times in the ten days around our launch, and may
  change again. Snapshot each revision to the Wayback Machine when it changes.
