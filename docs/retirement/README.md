# Microsoft Whiteboard retirement evidence

The site and the launch campaign state that Microsoft retires its standalone Whiteboard
app on **September 14, 2026**, citing
[Migration of Whiteboards from Azure to OneDrive](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/migration-of-whiteboards-from-azure-to-onedrive).
Microsoft edits that page as the wind-down proceeds, and the date has already been moved
out of the page's headline sections. This folder preserves the evidence behind the claim
so it stays citable regardless of future edits.

- [Microsoft Whiteboard shutdown (original date).png](<Microsoft Whiteboard shutdown (original date).png>) —
  the "Final migration and retirement schedule" table as originally published, with
  September 14, 2026 as its closing row: "The standalone Whiteboard apps for Windows,
  iOS, and Android retire. Whiteboard remains available through Microsoft Teams and the
  web."
- [Archived copy of that revision](https://web.archive.org/web/20260818052606/https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/migration-of-whiteboards-from-azure-to-onedrive)
  (Wayback Machine, August 18, 2026), where the September 14 date is clearly visible in
  the schedule table.

As of August 23, 2026 the live page still carries the sentence "The standalone Windows,
iOS, and Android apps retire on September 14, 2026", but only in passing under
"Migration trigger and eligible tenants". The schedule table now opens with the migration
deadlines instead, which Microsoft slipped by roughly a month (migration end
August 22 → September 25; permanent deletion September 5 → October 16; service shutdown
November 30 added) while the app retirement date held.

When citing the retirement publicly:

- Say the app **"retires"** — Microsoft's own verb. Do not claim the app *stops working*
  on September 14; installed copies may run for some time after.
- Always scope the claim to the standalone **app**: Whiteboard itself remains available
  in Teams and on the web.
- Quote the sentence verbatim, and prefer the archived copy or the bitmap over the live
  page, which can change again.
